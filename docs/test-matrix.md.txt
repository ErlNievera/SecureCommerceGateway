## Notes

- Products and orders use in-memory sample data. Data resets when the corresponding service restarts.
- The local test credentials are `admin` / `Pass123!`. These are development-only credentials and should not be used in production.
- JWTs expire after 30 minutes.
- Never commit access tokens or production secrets to source control.
- All listed endpoint status codes were observed during local testing.

\* **TC-11:** Confirm the first five rapid login attempts returned `200 OK` and the sixth returned `429 Too Many Requests` before final submission. A `429` response was observed during testing, 
but the complete six-request sequence should be verified to claim the exact threshold confidently.
